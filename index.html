WEEPING ANGELS

HOW TO PLAY

  • The core rule: the Angels move only when you are not looking at them. While an Angel is in your view, it is frozen — the instant it leaves your view, it snaps closer. Keep them in sight.
  • Controls: WASD to move, mouse to look, Esc to release the cursor (pauses the game).
  • Forced blink: every few seconds the screen blacks out for a moment — you can't stop it, and every Angel gets a free move while you're blind. Position yourself before it happens.
  • Danger zone: if any Angel reaches ~1.6 m of you, it's game over. The HUD shows the nearest Angel's distance and warns you when one is close.
  • Win: survive 90 seconds, or reach the green exit beacon. The exit is hidden from your start — you'll have to turn away from danger to find it.
  • Beware decoy statues: some figures never move. Only watching closely tells a statue from a stalking Angel.
